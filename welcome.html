How it works

From curious to connected in minutes

This directory is a starting point, not a destination. Find an organization whose work speaks to you, then reach out. The real work happens in the world.

01
Browse or filter
Search by name, filter by type of work, election phase, or the community you care about most. Every organization has a plain-language description of what they do.
02
Read the details
Click any organization to see their full profile — what they do, where they operate, who they serve, and how to get in touch.
03
Reach out
Contact the organization directly. Most welcome new volunteers and will train you for everything you need. No experience required.
04
Know an organization we missed?
Use the List your group tab to submit an organization you think belongs here. Submissions are reviewed and added regularly.
Ways to get involved

There's no wrong place to start

Elections involve a lot of moving parts. Here are some of the most common ways everyday people contribute.

📋
Voter registration
Help eligible citizens get on the rolls — at events, door to door, or online.
Find organizations →
📣
Get out the vote
Remind, encourage, and help registered voters actually make it to the polls.
Find organizations →
✉️
Ballot curing
Contact voters whose mail ballots were rejected and help them fix the problem before the deadline.
Find organizations →
🏫
Poll work
Serve as a poll worker (running the election) or poll watcher (observing for your party or organization).
Find organizations →
🔎
Voter roll monitoring
Help keep voter registration lists accurate — verifying records, flagging errors, and helping voters re-register.
Find organizations →
⚖️
Legal & advocacy
Use legal, research, or communications skills to support voting rights cases and policy reform.
